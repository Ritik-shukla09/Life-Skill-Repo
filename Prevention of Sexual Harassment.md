# Prevention of Sexual Harassment 

---

## Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes **any unwelcome conduct of a sexual nature** that affects a person’s dignity or creates a hostile, intimidating, or offensive environment. As explained in the MountBlue PoSH document and training videos, such behavior includes:

- **Physical behaviour**
  - Unwanted touching, hugging, patting, or physical advances
  - Blocking someone’s movement or invading personal space

- **Verbal behaviour**
  - Sexually coloured remarks or jokes
  - Comments about someone’s body, appearance, or personal life
  - Requests or demands for sexual favors, directly or indirectly

- **Non-verbal / visual behaviour**
  - Staring, leering, or making suggestive gestures
  - Displaying or sharing pornographic or sexually explicit material

- **Digital or online behaviour**
  - Inappropriate emails, messages, emojis, images, or video calls
  - Repeated unwanted communication with sexual intent

- **Quid Pro Quo harassment**
  - Linking professional benefits (promotion, appraisal, job security) to sexual favors

- **Hostile work environment**
  - Repeated or severe actions that make the workplace uncomfortable, unsafe, or intimidating

The **intention does not matter**; what matters is whether the behavior is **unwelcome**.

---

## Question 2: What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?

If I face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of sexual harassment, I would take the following actions, as guided by the PoSH policy and training resources:

1. **Recognize the behavior** as inappropriate and unacceptable.
2. **Communicate clearly** that the behavior is unwelcome, if it is safe to do so.
3. **Document the incident(s)** with details such as date, time, location, nature of behavior, and witnesses.
4. **Report the matter** to the organization’s **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** following the PoSH process.
5. **Support the affected person** if I am a witness, encourage reporting, and cooperate fully with the inquiry.
6. **Maintain confidentiality** and avoid gossip or retaliation.
7. **Escalate externally** (Local Committee or legal authorities) if internal mechanisms are unavailable or ineffective.

Timely reporting and responsible action help create a **safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace** for everyone.

---
## How to handle cases of harassment?

Cases of harassment should be handled promptly, sensitively, and as per the PoSH guidelines:

- **Take the complaint seriously** and listen without judgment.
- **Ensure safety and confidentiality** of the affected person.
- **Document the incident(s)** clearly, including dates, time, location, and witnesses.
- **Encourage reporting** to the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) as defined in the PoSH policy.
- **Follow due process** during inquiry — fair hearing to both parties, no bias, and timely resolution.
- **Avoid retaliation** against the complainant or witnesses.
- **Take appropriate corrective or disciplinary action** if the allegation is proven.
- **Provide support** (emotional, professional) to the affected person during and after the process.

Handling harassment responsibly helps maintain trust, dignity, and a safe workplace environment.

---

## How to behave appropriately?

Appropriate behavior in the workplace is based on respect, professionalism, and boundaries:

- **Treat everyone with dignity and respect**, regardless of gender or position.
- **Maintain professional boundaries** in communication, gestures, and interactions.
- **Avoid comments, jokes, or actions** that may be offensive, sexual, or discriminatory.
- **Respect personal space and consent** at all times.
- **Be mindful in digital communication** (emails, chats, video calls, social media).
- **Speak up or intervene responsibly** if you witness inappropriate behavior.
- **Follow organizational policies** and participate actively in PoSH awareness programs.

Appropriate behavior contributes to a **safe, inclusive, and respectful workplace culture**.
