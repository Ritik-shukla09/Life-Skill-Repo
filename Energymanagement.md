### **Question 1**  
**What are the activities you do that make you relax — Calm quadrant?**

**Answer:**  
- Listening to music  
- Taking a walk  
- Deep breathing or meditation  
- Spending time alone quietly  
- Light stretching or yoga  
- Talking to a trusted friend  
- Watching something peaceful  

---

### **Question 2**  
**When do you find yourself entering the Stress quadrant?**

**Answer:**  
- When deadlines are close  
- When learning something completely new  
- When multitasking  
- When expectations are unclear  

---

### **Question 3**  
**How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?**

**Answer:**  
- You feel energetic and motivated  
- You look forward to the task  
- You lose track of time while doing the activity  
- You feel curious and fully engaged  
- You sense positive anticipation about the outcome

### **Question 4**  
**Paraphrase the video “Sleep is your Superpower” in brief (only points).**

**Answer:**  
- Sleep strengthens the immune system  
- Sleep improves memory and learning  
- Poor sleep increases risk of diseases  
- Sleep enhances emotional stability  
- Deep sleep repairs the brain  
- Lack of sleep reduces lifespan  
- Sleep boosts creativity and problem solving  

---

### **Question 5**  
**What are some ideas you can implement to sleep better?**

**Answer:**  
- Maintain a fixed sleep schedule  
- Avoid screens 1 hour before bed  
- Keep the room cool and dark  
- Limit caffeine after afternoon  
- Do light stretching or relaxation before bed  
- Avoid heavy meals at night  
- Reduce stress through meditation or journaling

### **Question 6**  
**Paraphrase the video “Brain-Changing Benefits of Exercise” — minimum 5 points.**

**Answer:**  
- Exercise increases brain chemicals that improve mood  
- It enhances memory and learning ability  
- Exercise helps grow new brain cells (neurogenesis)  
- It protects the brain from stress and aging  
- Exercise improves focus and attention  
- Helps prevent cognitive decline  
- Boosts overall brain performance  

---

### **Question 7**  
**What steps can you take to exercise more?**

**Answer:**  
- Start with small, easy workouts (10–15 minutes)  
- Fix a daily exercise time  
- Choose activities you enjoy (walking, cycling, sports)  
- Keep workout clothes ready to reduce friction  
- Track progress to stay motivated  
- Mix different types of activities to avoid boredom
