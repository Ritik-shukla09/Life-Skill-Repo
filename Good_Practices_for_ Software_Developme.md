### Question 1: Which point(s) were new to you?

The points that were new and particularly insightful for me were:
- The importance of **over-communicating proactively**, especially informing the team early about delays, issues, or changes instead of waiting for problems to escalate.
- The emphasis on **asking good questions** by clearly explaining the problem, sharing what has already been tried, and using tools like screenshots, Loom videos, or GitHub gists to make it easier for others to help.
- The idea that **preserving attention is a skill** and can be improved deliberately through practices like deep work, time tracking, and minimizing distractions.

### Question 2: Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?

I need to improve most on **asking clear and effective questions** and **managing focus during work hours**.

To make progress in these areas, I plan to:
- Clearly structure my questions by describing the problem, sharing error messages, and listing the solutions I have already tried.
- Practice focused work by keeping my phone on silent, blocking social media during work hours, and following a fixed work routine.
- Track my time using a productivity app to better understand where my focus is being lost and improve gradually.

These steps should help me become more effective, communicative, and productive as a software developer.
