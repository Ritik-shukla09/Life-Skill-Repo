# Learning Process
**Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique?**

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you understand a concept by explaining it in simple words as if you were teaching it to someone else.

**Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

Active and diffused modes are two ways the brain processes information. The active mode is when you concentrate intensely on a problem, while the diffused mode works in the background when your mind is relaxed.

* Active Mode: Focused thinking used for solving problems, studying, and practicing.

* Diffused Mode: Relaxed thinking used for creativity, insights, and connecting ideas.

* Both modes together make learning faster and deeper.
