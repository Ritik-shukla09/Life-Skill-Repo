
## Question 1  
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

**Answer:**  
Grit is the ability to stay committed to long-term goals even when progress is slow and challenges appear. The video explains that perseverance and passion are more important for success than natural talent.

## Question 2  
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

**Answer:**  
A growth mindset is the belief that skills and intelligence can improve through effort and learning. The video shows that challenges strengthen the brain and help people reach their full potential.

---



## Question 3  
**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

**Answer:**  
Internal Locus of Control means believing that your actions, choices, and effort determine your success—not luck or external events.  
The key point of the video is that motivation grows when you focus on what you can control rather than blaming outside circumstances.

## Question 4  
**What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset (explanation not needed)?**

**Answer:**  
- Embrace challenges  
- Learn from mistakes  
- Seek feedback  
- Practice consistently  
- Celebrate small improvements  
- Replace “I can’t” with “I can learn this”  
- Focus on progress, not perfection  
- Stay persistent  
- View effort as the path to mastery  
- Surround yourself with positive influences
